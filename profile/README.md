# Silus

**Protect what's most important.**

Silus builds security products and delivers services for teams that can't afford to get it wrong, across enterprise IT and the operational technology behind critical infrastructure.

### Services

- **Infrastructure & Hardening**: secure design, deployment, and hardening of enterprise systems, from everyday networks to airgapped and classified environments
- **Risk & Threat Analysis**: find what's actually exploitable, map the attack paths adversaries would use, and turn it into decisions leadership can act on
- **Consulting & Compliance**: security programs and roadmaps that hold up under scrutiny, plus audit-ready mapping for NIST RMF, CMMC, NERC CIP, HIPAA, and PCI DSS
- **Critical Infrastructure & OT**: ICS assessments, NERC CIP, and IT/OT segmentation that keeps enterprise threats away from control networks
- **Penetration Testing & Red Teaming**: network, web app, and social engineering testing through objective-based red team engagements
- **Cyber Ranges & Labs**: isolated environments to train, exercise, and break things before they reach production
